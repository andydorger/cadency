About Us

Operators and investors

Cadency is a private holding company. We are operators and investors: we invest our own capital and stay involved in running the businesses we own.

On the name

In heraldry, cadency is the system that marks each son of a family on the shared coat of arms. The eldest bears the label, the bar of three points across the head of our shield. It marks what we intend to be: one house, carried forward with care.

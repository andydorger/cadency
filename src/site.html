About Us

Operators and investors

Cadency is a private holding company. We are operators and investors: we invest our own capital and stay involved in running the businesses we own.

On the name

In heraldry, cadency is the system that marks each son of a family on the shared coat of arms. The eldest bears the label, the bar of three points across the head of our shield. Below it sits the talbot, the old English hound, kept for loyalty and a long memory. Together they describe what we intend to be: one house, carried forward with care.
